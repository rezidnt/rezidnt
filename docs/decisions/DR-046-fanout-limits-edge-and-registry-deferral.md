## Context

**Item 1 — a consequence neither record anticipated.** DR-045 §Decision 1 admits only a verified agent macaroon on `fan_out`. DR-017 §Decision 6 mints the root key **per process, never persisted** (`bins/rezidentd/src/runs.rs:187`, doc comment `:160-166`; verifier wiring `bins/rezidentd/src/main.rs:304`). Composed: a lead's badge dies with the daemon, so **no `fan_out` call is admissible after a restart under any implementation**. The oracle's `fan_out_is_idempotent_per_task_across_a_daemon_restart` (`bins/rezidentd/tests/fan_out_live_e2e.rs:487`) re-presents the pre-restart `lead_badge` at `:523` — not failing, **unsatisfiable**. Provenance: DR-044 §Decision 1 requires only "a retry with the same keys re-returns the same runs and spawns nothing new"; the across-a-restart framing came from the orchestrator's brief to the oracle, not the record. The dedup map itself *does* survive a restart — it is log-derived from `agent.spawned.idempotency_key` (`runs.rs:149`, `:287`). Only the badge cannot.

**Item 2 — the lead→sub edge is mis-modelled (`/debrief` lane-2 fail; the slice's most serious finding).** The ontology fixes `permit.delegated` as a fact about *attenuation*: "a lead agent's badge **was attenuated** … to mint a child badge" (`spec/ontology.md:449`), `parent_badge_id` is "the badge **that was attenuated**" (`:451`), `child_badge_id` "the child **minted by the attenuation**" (`:452`), `added_caveats` "the predicates that make the child strictly ⊆ the parent — the monotonicity invariant" (`:453`). On the fan-out path the sub's badge is a **fresh `Macaroon::mint` against the daemon root key over the SUB's own run** (`runs.rs:802`); the lead's token never enters the daemon (it holds only `LeadDelegation { lead_run, lead_badge_id }`, `:672-678`). Nothing is attenuated, and the child stands in no derivation relation to the parent. The emitted `added_caveats: []` (`:1091`) therefore asserts a delegation that narrowed nothing — an *unnarrowed capability handoff*, a stronger false claim than silence. The implementation's own comment concedes the strain: "the ontology's `added_caveats` is a REQUIRED field, making the empty list the only truthful value available" (`:1071-1076`). This is I3, not naming: `DelegationRecord` is documented as an attenuation chain (`crates/rezidnt-state/src/lib.rs:98-107`), `AgentRunState.delegations` as "each attenuation-and-handoff at a sub-agent spawn earns one record" (`:444-452`), the reducer arm folds it (`:1070`), and `BoardRow.delegated` is **delegation-chain depth** from `delegations.len()` (`:1397-1399`, `:1457`). A lead that fans out to five subs now shows chain depth 5 and five attenuation records on the dossier `debrief` reads — none of which occurred. DR-042 §Decision 6 reserved exactly this case: a new subject is minted only when the graph genuinely cannot be folded from what exists, and only by the warden. That condition is now **demonstrated rather than assumed** — the existing fact cannot carry this event without lying about it.

**Item 3 — the registry wiring, and why the ruling flipped.** The daemon's spawn path calls its own minimal git-CLI `allocate_worktree` (`runs.rs:740`, defined `:1433-1463`) and publishes `worktree.allocated` directly (`:762-778`); the `GitAdapter` registry and its double-claim guard (`crates/rezidnt-adapters/git/src/lib.rs:777-796`) are unreached. Stronger: **no crate depends on `rezidnt-adapter-git`** (`bins/rezidentd/Cargo.toml:8-28`; the sole daemon mention is a doc comment, `runs.rs:756`). The owner's first instinct was to wire it in this slice, and this record was first drafted that way. It flipped on **new evidence, not on cost**: (a) the oracle demonstrated the owed I6 conflict test **cannot be written black-box even with the registry wired** — fan-out worktree paths are ULID-derived (`runs.rs:1438-1442`), so no test can pre-claim the path a task will take and two fan-out tasks can never collide with each other; making it testable needs an injectable allocation seam or a test-visible path, i.e. a *daemon* change, so the wiring's principal benefit does not arrive with the wiring; (b) the conflict-specific obligations are already machine-checked where a deterministic oracle exists (`crates/rezidnt-adapters/git/tests/worktree_conflict.rs`, `restart_and_discovery.rs`); (c) the repoint is an I3 hazard on the golden path's spawn route (below), and this slice now carries an open `/debrief` fail (Item 2) that must not queue behind S2 integration work.

**Strongest counterargument (recorded, not resolved away).** Deferring means shipping while a ratified record and a ratified ontology paragraph both describe a guard the path does not have — the precise failure mode this project says is worse than a long slice, and the reason the owner's first instinct was to wire it. The honest answer is not that the objection is wrong, but that wiring now buys the *description* of a guard without buying a *test* of it, at the cost of an I3 hazard on the golden path while a lane is red. So the deferral is paid for in writing: the falsehood is named here, the ontology correction is owed to the warden, and the wiring is a named slice rather than an open intention.

## Invariant posture

**I3 — LOAD-BEARING, and currently violated in derived state** by Item 2: `delegations` and `BoardRow.delegated` carry attenuations that never happened. **I4 — REUSE when the wiring lands**: routing onto `RepoSubstrate`/`GitAdapter` is use of an existing trait and its shipped implementation — no new substrate, no new backend, no new external dependency. **I6 — LOAD-BEARING**: DR-044 §Decision 3's refused-sub rule stands, but is unreached today and untestable black-box; recorded, not quietly dropped. **I1, I2, I5, I7, I8 — UNTOUCHED.**

## Decision

1. **Keep DR-017's ephemeral root key.** Persisting it would convert a process-lifetime secret into a long-lived stealable one to satisfy a requirement no record stated. DR-017 §Decision 6 stands unamended.
2. **Name the limitation: `fan_out` is structurally same-process-only.** A lead run that outlives a daemon restart **cannot fan out again** — its badge no longer verifies, and a fresh badge today means a fresh spawn. Anyone restarting a daemon mid-orchestration should expect this. It is a consequence of two accepted records, now on the record rather than latent.
3. **Re-cut the restart leg.** It asserts what DR-044 §Decision 1 actually requires — same keys, same runs, no new `agent.spawned` on the log, **same process**. The restart dimension stays covered by `spawn_key_idempotency_survives_daemon_restart`. A compensating leg is owed: a post-restart `fan_out` with a pre-restart lead badge is refused as an invalid badge and emits no `worktree.allocated` and no `agent.spawned`.
4. **WITHDRAW DR-044 §Decision 2b's lead-parented `permit.delegated` emit** (`runs.rs:1077-1096`). A fan-out is not an attenuation, and this fact cannot express it without asserting a narrowing that did not occur. Emitting nothing is strictly better than emitting a false attenuation — but it leaves `orchestration_graph` unable to fold the edge, which is why Decision 5 follows immediately rather than eventually. **Sequencing (ORCHESTRATOR'S RULING, recorded so it is not mistaken for a detail):** the withdrawal and its replacement land in the SAME commit, after the Decision 5 mint. The tree is never in a state where the graph is silent, and — more importantly — the withdrawal is never used as cover for shipping a slice with no edge at all. If the mint proves harder than expected, the honest outcome is that this slice stays open, NOT that the emit is quietly left in place; retaining a known-false fact for continuity would be a deliberate choice to keep lying, and is refused here.
5. **One warden `/subject` is OWED to mint the right fact — named here, NOT designed here** (DR-044 §Decision 2b's own "that is a warden question — flagged, not invented" governs). What the fact must express: *a lead run spawned a sub run*, keyed so `orchestration_graph` can fold the lead→sub edge without asserting any attenuation, with the sub end tied to the value `agent.spawned.badge_id` actually carries (DR-044's structural-identity requirement survives the withdrawal — if the two diverge the graph silently reports `fan_out: 0`). Followers, not warden work: the reducer (`crates/rezidnt-state/src/lib.rs:1070`) and `orchestration_graph` (`:1659`) both move to the new fact.
6. **The DR-017 self-edge stays valid and untouched.** A role-declaring spawn genuinely attenuates the base badge (`runs.rs:813-817`) and emits a true `permit.delegated` (`:1032-1052`). The projection's lane-1 `sub_run != lead_run` guard already declines to read it as orchestration. Nothing about that path changes.
7. **Considered and NOT taken: making the sub's badge a genuine attenuation of the lead's macaroon.** It would have made `permit.delegated` true as typed and given the sub strictly narrower authority than its lead — arguably the better security model and closer to DR-017/DR-018's intent. Not taken because its feasibility is **unverified**: the daemon holds the lead's badge *id*, not its token (`runs.rs:672-678`), and offline attenuation requires the token. Recorded so a future record can pick it up knowingly rather than rediscover it.
8. **The registry wiring is DEFERRED to its own named slice, and that slice converges BOTH allocation paths together** — fan-out and ordinary. Moving both at once dissolves the split-path problem rather than creating it: a registry that sees only fan-out allocations cannot guard a fan-out racing an ordinary spawn, so a fan-out-only wiring buys less than it appears to. **The brief for that slice is fixed here** (all verified against the tree): the daemon must take its first dependency on `rezidnt-adapter-git` (`bins/rezidentd/Cargo.toml:8-28` lacks it); `WorktreeReq` has **no principal field** (`git/src/lib.rs:125-132`) and `alloc_worktree` **hardcodes `"rezidnt"`** into both payload (`:809`) and `RegistryEntry` (`:823`), so it cannot emit the ratified `run:<ULID>` until a principal field is threaded (internal Rust, additive, default `"rezidnt"` so every existing caller and its green tests are unchanged); the adapter's `emit` (`:255-276`) builds events with a **`None` workspace envelope and its own correlation and broadcasts on a channel instead of appending to the fabric**, so a naive repoint drops the allocation fact off the log (I3); the adapter **emits its own `worktree.allocated`** (`:810-813`), so repointing without silencing one side **double-emits** — exactly one fact per allocation, carrying the envelope `workspace` and the vet causation as `runs.rs:762-778` does today, is the non-negotiable condition; and the two path layouts differ (`<repo>/.rezidnt/worktrees/<agent>-<run>`, `runs.rs:1438-1442`, vs `<repo-parent>/<repo>-wt-<name>`, `git/src/lib.rs:637-658`), so the repoint changes on-disk locations. The slice should also land the **injectable allocation seam** Item 3(a) shows the I6 conflict test needs.
9. **A distinct worktree-conflict refusal code is OWED to that slice, not minted here.** DR-044 §Decision 3 says a conflicted task's outcome "carries the conflict code", but no such code exists — the `codes` module runs `badge.required` … `fan_out.lead_only` (`crates/rezidnt-mcp/src/lib.rs:52-95`) and every allocation refusal collapses to `SPAWN_FAILED` (`:75`). A caller cannot distinguish "the tree was contended, retry with the same keys" from "this spawn is broken". Minting it now would be minting a code no path can emit; it becomes actionable exactly when Decision 8 lands.

## Consequences

- **Roadmap (§19/§16).** The DR-044 build slice narrows: Item 2 is its blocking work (a warden `/subject`, then reducer + projection follow-through), Item 1 is a test re-cut, and the registry wiring leaves this slice as a **new named slice** — registry convergence, both paths, plus the allocation seam and the conflict code. Phase 3's terminal-substrate arc is untouched.
- **Risk register.** ADDS: (1) *derived-state contamination, live now* — `BoardRow.delegated` and `AgentRunState.delegations` overstate attenuation chains for every fanned-out lead until Decisions 4–5 land; this is on the dossier `debrief` reads. (2) *DR-044 §Decision 3's conflict semantics remain unreached for fan-out* — isolation today holds **by ULID uniqueness of the worktree path, not by any registry guard**; the refused-sub rule describes a mechanism the path does not have, and says so here rather than in a comment. (3) *`spec/ontology.md:216` remains false of the code* — it states a lead "requests one through the same `RepoSubstrate::allocate` path, under the same registry"; correcting that text is owed to the warden pass, because a ratified paragraph asserting an untaken path is exactly the failure this record exists to stop compounding. (4) *the future repoint is the highest-blast-radius change in the arc* — canaries are `bins/rezidentd/tests/golden_path.rs`, `bins/rezidentd/tests/open_flow.rs:63` (the `"rezidnt"` value pin), `crates/rezidnt-state/tests/s2_worktrees.rs`, and the adapter's five suites.
- **Test/criterion honesty, plain words.** This record **weakens one test and says so**: the restart leg of `fan_out_live_e2e.rs:487` drops an across-a-restart assertion no record required and no implementation can satisfy — a real reduction in what the suite claims, traded for a leg that can be true, with the Decision 3 compensating leg owed rather than optional. It also **defers an owed guard**: DR-044 §Consequences (e)'s I6 conflict test stays unwritten, and this record states why it could not be written even under the original ruling — the ULID-derived path gives a black-box test nothing to pre-claim. Nothing else is relaxed; no verifier bar moves and no verdict is coerced. Owed guards: (a) the re-cut same-process idempotency leg; (b) the post-restart-refusal leg; (c) a guard on the new subject once minted — a fanned-out lead's `delegations` and `BoardRow.delegated` show **zero** attenuation records, while `orchestration_graph` still folds its subs; (d) the allocator-value pin — nothing machine-checks `run:<ULID>` on fan-out (`fan_out_live_e2e.rs:44-48`, `:193-194` explicitly decline), while `"rezidnt"` on an ordinary spawn **is** pinned (`open_flow.rs:63`) and must survive the future repoint; (e) the I6 conflict test, deferred with the wiring; (f) DR-045's owed operator-badge refusal guard, still outstanding.
- **Deferred, named.** (a) The registry-convergence slice of Decision 8, with its seam and its conflict code. (b) The genuine-attenuation badge model of Decision 7. (c) A re-badging path for a long-lived lead — the only thing that would lift Decision 2's limitation; no demonstrated demand, and it must not become root-key persistence by another name. (d) Nested and cross-workspace fan-out stay out, per DR-044.

Amendments to this record require DR-047.
