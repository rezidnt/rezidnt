## Context

The slice landed across six commits: `d56bcc7` (adapter foundation — `Allocator`, `FactSink`, `WorktreeReq.orphan`, the layout move), `e0e226a` (the daemon repoint — `Daemon::repo_adapter`, `FabricSink`, the retirement of the private allocator), `e8283f5` (the owed guards), and three remediation passes `58dc447`, `b1002fb`, `8e1ef5c`. Host `/vet` green, `/debrief` pass, `origin/main` at `8e1ef5c`.

**The brief was a prediction, and this is the record's most useful content.** DR-046 §Decision 8 said its brief was verified against the tree. It was not. (i) A **hard blocker it did not see**: `REGISTRY_PATH` was `.rezidnt/worktrees`, a JSONL *file* at the exact path where the daemon `create_dir_all`s a *directory*. Mutually exclusive — `GitAdapter::open` would have `read_to_string`'d a directory on any repo the daemon had opened, so the repoint could not land as briefed. (ii) "Additive … every existing caller and its green tests are unchanged" is **false**: five exhaustive `WorktreeReq` struct literals, every one edited. (iii) The brief names the two allocators' *layout* difference but not the *behavioral* one — `rezidnt-testkit::make_project` builds a HEAD-less repo that can serve neither `worktree add --detach` nor `-b`; losing the empty-repo orphan fallback would have broken roughly eight daemon suites (now `WorktreeReq.orphan`). (iv) Three more omissions: the **CAS split** (`GitAdapter::open` opened its own `Cas` while `Daemon` holds an `Arc<Cas>`, so an adapter-minted `diff_summary` ref would be unresolvable from the daemon — closed by `open_with_cas`); **`startup_facts`** (the on-open reconciliation runs *inside* `open`, before a sink can attach, so those facts would never have reached the log — drained explicitly in `repo_adapter`); and that the daemon needs the allocation fact's **id** for `agent.spawned` causation. Recorded as the arc's continuing lesson and not as blame: **a record that says "verified against the tree" is still a prediction until a build meets it.**

The distinction matters and is kept sharp throughout: exactly **one** stated premise was false; the other five were **omissions** — things the brief never claimed either way. That is the more serious failure, not the lesser one. A brief that omits a hard blocker is more dangerous than one that misstates a detail, because an omission reads as absence of risk.

**Strongest counterargument (recorded, not resolved away).** Decision 4 leaves two live emitters of one subject, which reads like exactly the double-emit DR-046 forbade — and the daemon's own gate-time fact ships carrying `source: "rezidnt-adapter-git"`, a wire-visible attribution of a fact the adapter did not mint, in golden fixtures, ahead of the ontology that describes it. The honest answer is not that the objection is wrong: it is that the two facts are two *observations*, not two records of one occurrence, and that the alternative — letting `pre_merge` inherit a watcher fact from a race — trades a disclosed duplication for an I6 violation. The price is paid in writing here and owed to the warden, not resolved by asserting it away.

## Invariant posture

**I3 — LOAD-BEARING and now honored where it was not.** Adapter facts reach the log through the injected `FactSink` (`FabricSink`), whose `Err` fails the allocation that minted it: a broadcast is not an append. Decision 5 records a *derived-state* defect the repoint introduced and this slice fixed. **I4 — PRESERVED, both directions.** The adapter still takes no `rezidnt-fabric` dependency — the sink is a trait it holds and never resolves; the daemon's new edge runs adapter-ward only, behind `DynRepoSubstrate` with an injection seam (`Daemon::repo_substrate`). **I6 — LOAD-BEARING.** `pre_merge` pins the ref it is about to verify synchronously; gate inputs are fixed at gate time, never inherited from a race. `GitError::Conflict { path, holder }` is a variant a caller *matches*, never a message it substring-searches. **I1, I2, I5, I7, I8 — UNTOUCHED.**

## Decision

1. **Record DR-046 §Decision 8's brief as DISCHARGED, item by item.** The daemon took its first `rezidnt-adapter-git` dependency in production `[dependencies]`; `WorktreeReq` carries a principal (`Allocator`, closed to `Rezidnt | Run(Ulid)`); the adapter's facts are appended via `FactSink`; exactly one `worktree.allocated` per allocation, the adapter being sole emitter; both allocation paths converged on `RepoSubstrate::alloc_worktree` through `Daemon::repo_adapter`, one adapter per canonicalized repo root; and the allocation seam landed. §Decision 9's `codes::WORKTREE_CONFLICT` is minted and mapped. DR-044 §Decision 3's refused-sub rule is **reached for the first time** — a conflicted task is refused alone and its siblings still spawn. DR-046 §Consequences guard (e), the I6 conflict test, is **discharged**.
2. **Record the falsified premises above as findings of this record**, so the next brief is read as a hypothesis. No process is minted; the obligation is the one the arc already carries — check a record's premises against the tree before building to it.
3. **Ratify the two in-slice layout rulings** (made at DEFAULT level; no `/dr` was required, recorded here so they are findable). `REGISTRY_PATH` → `.rezidnt/registry.jsonl`; `derive_worktree_path` → the daemon's in-repo `<repo>/.rezidnt/worktrees/<name>` — **the adapter moved to the daemon's layout, not the reverse**, because `.gitignore` already designates `.rezidnt/` as the home for daemon-materialized worktrees, because it preserves shipped v0.0.1 on-disk behavior, and because it saves two existing test levers (`spec_init_open_e2e`'s tempdir confinement, `fan_out_live_e2e`'s `block_allocations`). No migration path is owed: no crate depended on the adapter. The registry file deliberately sits **outside** the directory `block_allocations` chmods.
4. **`diff.ready` has TWO emitters, and that is deliberate.** The repoint put the notify watcher on the golden path for the first time. Measured: a 50 ms harness gap yields one fact, 700 ms yields two, from both sources. They are different observations — the watcher's is trailing-edge debounced, deduplicated, detached and droppable; the gate-time fact is the synchronously pinned input `pre_merge` verifies. Guarded by an ownership test with a non-vacuity leg, plus a host-side structural backstop that the daemon mints the subject at exactly one site. **OWED to a warden `/subject`:** the ontology's `diff.ready` emitter cell names the watcher only, and the daemon's gate-time fact carries `source: "rezidnt-adapter-git"` (also the source on `diff.merged`). The attribution moves **with** the ontology, not ahead of it.
5. **Record the post-merge clobber, and the fix, because of HOW it hid.** `merge_worktree` runs `git add -A` + commit inside the still-watched tree. Those commands write nothing under the watched path — a linked worktree's index and HEAD live in its private gitdir, its objects and branch ref in the common dir — but they **read** every tracked file, and inotify arms `IN_OPEN`, so the reads woke the debounce loop; 250 ms later the now-clean tree summarized to a 26-byte header-only fact that overwrote `last_diff` on a worktree already folded `"merged"`. **Host `/vet` could never have caught it:** `ReadDirectoryChangesW` has no open/read notification — Windows measured 0 events, WSL 1. Fixed with an `is_change_event` predicate: reads do not wake the loop. **Considered and not taken:** dropping the watch at merge — the merge burst is entirely reads so nothing is lost by filtering, and `release_worktree` would emit `worktree.released`, folding `"released"` over `"merged"` and trading one derived-state regression for another. A second measured consequence, also closed: `summarize_to_cas`'s own reads re-armed the watch that fired it — a gix status walk plus a blake3 every 250 ms per worktree, forever, invisible because `last_hash` kept repeats off the log.
6. **Name the genuinely open item as the next slice: the worktree release lifecycle.** The daemon calls `release_worktree` from **no production path**, so DR-007's ratified allocate→use→release lifecycle never completes: every allocation leaks a `notify` watcher plus a debounce task for the process lifetime, trees stay on disk, and registry entries are never closed. Acceptable at golden-path cardinality; not at fleet scale. It is genuinely undecided, not merely unbuilt — releasing at merge trades one regression for another (Decision 5) — so that slice must settle whether a merged worktree is retained, what `worktree.released` does to a `"merged"` fold, whether a failed run's tree survives for triage, and the `WorktreeId` threading `RunTaskContext` lacks. **Named here, not designed here.**
7. **Say explicitly what this record does not touch.** DR-017 §Decision 6's ephemeral root key; DR-045 in whole; DR-046 §Decisions 1–7; and DR-001's sole-allocator model, whose two-allocator reconciliation problem stays deleted — one writer, whichever path performs the write — now guarded by the registry the model always described. No plan-body text is superseded, so no "amended by DR-047" pointer is owed under any §-heading; the mechanism correction lives in the ontology, already landed.
8. **Standing lesson, ruled here so it is citable: remediation prose is the highest-risk prose in the repo.** The arc's ledger is eleven instances of one class — a record, doc, comment, or test header asserting a mechanism the code lacks: four in DR-044/045/046, four found during this build, the ontology inversion (corrected in the morning, falsified by this slice the same afternoon), the adapter's own four-case header, and — the sharpest — an eleventh introduced **by a remediation commit written to end the pattern**, in the very sentence its author reported catching. Plus a false caveat inside the correction of a false caveat (the `gate.passed` ratification, third order). **Three of the eleven were introduced by remediation commits.** "I checked my own writing" is evidence that the pass happened, not that it was complete.

## Consequences

- **Roadmap (§19/§16).** DR-046 §Consequences (a) — the registry-convergence slice — is **closed**. The next named slice is the worktree release lifecycle (Decision 6). One warden `/subject` is queued (Decision 4). Phase 3's terminal-substrate arc is untouched and remains the other, larger line.
- **Risk register.** RETIRES: "DR-044 §Decision 3's conflict semantics remain unreached" and "isolation holds by ULID uniqueness, not by any registry guard" — both are now false in the good direction. ADDS: (1) *watcher and registry-entry leak per allocation*, unbounded over daemon lifetime, until Decision 6's slice lands. (2) *a `source` attribution shipped ahead of its ontology* — wire-visible and in golden fixtures, deliberate and disclosed, owed to the warden. (3) *a whole class of watcher behavior is WSL-only* — `ReadDirectoryChangesW` cannot produce read events, so the defect in Decision 5 and any successor are structurally invisible to host `/vet`.
- **Test/criterion honesty, plain words.** **Nothing was weakened to reach green, and no verifier bar moved.** Two guards are disclosed as **test-after-implementation**: the `startup_facts` drain and the plumbing of the C7 sibling-proceed leg. The `is_change_event` coverage is **narrowed, not closed** — host `/vet` judges the predicate; it never judges the watch loop's *use* of it, so deleting the guard clause leaves the unit test green while only WSL boards go red. **C8 (the canaries) stands `inconclusive` on the `/debrief` side across four passes** and is not coerced: it is a claim about executed outcomes that a read-only checker cannot discharge, and it belongs to `/vet`, which executes and reported pass. Two assertions that had gone tautological were **restored** to read from an independent record and are strictly stronger than what they replaced.
- **Non-blocking residues the closing `/debrief` named.** "refs" in the private-gitdir sentence is imprecise (only HEAD/bisect/worktree/rewritten are per-worktree; the branch ref lives in the common dir); the "only WSL boards go red" claim understates host coverage (a `dead_code` lint would trip); and one handoff bullet rules a two-clause caveat "confirmed false" while substantiating one clause.
- **Deferred, named.** (a) The worktree release lifecycle (Decision 6). (b) The warden pass of Decision 4. (c) DR-046's own carry-overs are unchanged: the genuine-attenuation badge model, a re-badging path for a long-lived lead, nested and cross-workspace fan-out.
