## Decision

1. **The `agent.completed.error?` mint is DISCLOSED and ratified as additive.** One optional payload field on a ratified v1 subject: `v` stays 1, existing fixtures lack the key and stay valid unchanged, and the field carries a DR-012 declared-vs-absent ruling (present IFF the harness reported a reason; omitted, never `null`). Its I2 length bound (`ERROR_MESSAGE_CAP`, 1 KiB, enforced once for every substrate in `Completion::into_fact`) and its length-disambiguated elision marker are ratified as recorded in `spec/ontology.md`. No new authorization is created here — the field is built, tested, warden-recorded and already relied upon; what was missing is the paper. *(Alternative — treat the warden changelog entry as sufficient disclosure and mint no record — REJECTED: the ontology records what the taxonomy IS; the decision records record what a slice CHANGED and why. DR-052 §Context item (e) already declined this same shortcut for `gate.failed.worktree?`, and accepting it here would split the discipline across two records for no reason but fatigue.)*

2. **The `cost.input_tokens?`/`output_tokens?` optionality ruling is DISCLOSED as part of the same mint.** Present-or-absent together; absence is honest and folds to `None` end-to-end; zeros would be a present claim of a measurement never made — reading, in `spec/ontology.md`'s words, "as a free run." Named here because DR-048 slice C will build a second, larger consumer of these fields and no record previously carried the ruling. **Scoped deliberately:** this is a claim about cost ACCOUNTING fidelity, not about selection — DR-048's v1 selection admits only PASSING candidates and its v1 comparison is verdicts + wall time, so no token reading can hand a failed candidate a win. *(Alternative — fold this into a future slice-C record, where the collator that reads the fields actually gets built — REJECTED: the ruling was minted by the lane this record is already disclosing, on the same field-set, in the same warden entry; splitting one mint across two records to align with a consumer not yet built is how the original disclosure gap opened. Naming it now costs a sentence; re-finding it later costs another audit pass.)*

3. **DR-050 §Deferred's falsified prediction is CORRECTED, strike-preserved.** On acceptance, the clause gains an inline strike and a pointer recording that the work did reveal an ontology change — two, in fact — and naming them. DR-050's Context and Decision text stay UNCHANGED, consistent with its own historical-account framing. *(Alternative — leave it, since "flagged not assumed" was already the honest posture — REJECTED: the honesty of a prediction at drafting time does not survive its falsification unrecorded. A reader checking whether the Trials arc touched the taxonomy would find a record saying "none is expected" and stop.)*

4. **NO further ledger is amended by this record — the candidates are NAMED, not corrected.** Two drafts of this decision tried to widen a second record's four-limb ledger, first [DR-051](DR-051-codex-failure-recording-and-fallback-fidelity.md)'s and then [DR-050](DR-050-trials-slice-a-closeout.md)'s. Both are withdrawn, and the reason is worth recording because it is the substantive finding: **neither ledger is demonstrably false.**

   DR-051's own diff is paperwork, so under the record-scoped reading [DR-052](DR-052-worktree-release-ledger-correction.md) applies to its own self-ledger ("This record itself mints…"), its sentence is TRUE. DR-050's is true on its own terms too: its §Deferred explicitly places "the failing-transcript codex fixture" and "any ontology change the failing-transcript work might reveal" OUTSIDE its slice, and the mint landed at `4ceccb7`, posterior to slice A's closeout `a559623` — so the field was minted by work DR-050 deferred, not by the slice DR-050 closes. Its slice-scoped ledger does not become false by describing a slice that did not contain the mint.

   The second draft reached its verdict by applying slice-scope to DR-050 (making it false) and record-scope to DR-051 (making it true) — **selecting the reading that produced the amendment in one case and the exemption in the other.** That is an equivocation, and naming the ambiguity while relying on it would have been worse than not noticing it. *(Alternative — RESOLVE the scoping question here, ruling that a ledger describes the diff its record authorizes or closes — REJECTED, though it is probably the right rule: it would retroactively re-read every existing ledger including DR-049's, whose falsity DR-052 has already ratified, and a convention that reaches back into a ratified finding is not something to settle as a sub-clause of a disclosure record. It is named below as owed its own record. Alternative — widen DR-050 anyway on the theory that a closeout record should account for work done between its slice and its writing — REJECTED: that is a new obligation invented to justify an amendment already drafted, which is the tail wagging the dog.)*

5. **The four-limb ledger form is RETIRED as a house DEFAULT — on a premise that weakened under review, stated at its surviving strength.** The evidence is now **exactly two data points, only one of which is a false ledger**: (i) DR-049 §Amends, false because the slice minted a field the sentence had no slot for — already ratified as false by DR-052 item (e), not a new finding here; and (ii) DR-052 §Amends' own self-ledger, written on the pass that was correcting this very form, which reads "NO invariant, NO subject, NO field, NO new dep" — four limbs with the TRAIT-METHOD limb missing. Nothing in (ii) is false; it is an author actively remediating the form and still not reproducing it. **Two earlier drafts of this record claimed two false ledgers; Decision 4 above withdraws the second, and the count is corrected here rather than carried.** The DEFAULT form gains the missing slot — **invariant · subject · FIELD · trait method · dep** — and a record predicting "no ontology change is expected" owes a re-check of that prediction before ratification.

   **The honest weight:** one false ledger across fifty-three records does not by itself establish a systemic form defect, and a reader who thinks this decision is premature is not making a mistake. What carries it, at reduced strength, is that the form is demonstrably hard to reproduce even under active attention (ii), that the fix is purely additive — one slot, no existing ledger invalidated — and that DEFAULT status means dropping it costs a note if the next ten records show the form was never the problem. *(Alternative — treat both as drafting slips and add no convention — a LIVE and respectable option now that the count is one; it loses only on the additive-and-cheap-to-revisit argument above, not on weight of evidence, and it would win outright if the change were BINDING. Alternative — DEFER the form retirement to its own record, DR-054 — REJECTED, though close: the evidence for the form defect IS this record's findings, and splitting them means DR-054 argues from a summary; the arc's history is that a deferred paperwork item is the one that does not get written. Alternative — mint a verifier that greps ontology changelog entries against record ledgers — REJECTED as premature: a verifier over prose is not deterministic in the I6 sense.)*
