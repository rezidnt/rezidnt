## Context

Slice A is done and correct on its own terms — claude-code stayed green, the codex adapter and `model` field landed inside `crates/rezidnt-run` behind the DR-048 §Decision 6 file boundary, never touching `runs.rs`. Getting there surfaced three findings out of slice A's scope that must not be lost.

**1. The version gate is a well-tested unreachable.** `version_gate`/`codex_version_gate` (`crates/rezidnt-run/src/adapter.rs:167,173`) have zero non-test callers — only the two trait impls (`:385,590`), themselves called only by tests. The adjacent vet native (`crates/rezidnt-gate/src/lib.rs:525-551`) requires an agent spec to *declare* `harness_version` but never compares it to a tested list, and nothing asks the spawned binary its actual version. No spawn is gated on harness version; the pin that exists is a self-declared string never verified against the binary. Slice A hardened this gate's rule (codex gates at MAJOR.MINOR, `TESTED_CODEX_VERSIONS = &[(0,145)]`) — correct work on a mechanism nothing currently calls. The daemon still only admits `"claude-code"` (`SUPPORTED_HARNESSES`, `bins/rezidentd/src/runs.rs:685`), so this is dormant, not yet live-dangerous.

**2. Three daemon-side traps, all in `bins/rezidentd/src/runs.rs`, none fixable from slice A's crate (out of its BINDING scope per DR-048 §Decision 6; the file was concurrently owned by DR-049's lane):**
   - `:1007`/`:1192` — `agent.spawned.pep = "enforced"` is stamped from `agent.gates.iter().any(|g| g == "permit")`, not from `plan.permit_hook_config().is_some()`. For claude-code the two agree, hiding the mis-keying. `SpawnPlan::for_codex` (`crates/rezidnt-run/src/spawner.rs:79`) now returns `Result` and refuses a declared `[gates.permit]` outright — slice A closed the trap from the crate side — but the daemon still derives the stamp from the wrong signal, and a future substrate whose PEP-equivalent isn't gate-name-shaped will spring it.
   - `:1665-1674` — the stream loop tolerates `map_line` errors by design ("a harness emitting a garbage line must not kill the run") and continues past a `tracing::warn`. Slice A added `AdapterError::ContractViolated` (`adapter.rs:82`), a categorically different animal from `BadLine` — one says "this line was garbage," the other says "the contract this adapter's facts rest on is false." Wired naively, the adapter refuses loudly and the daemon whispers.
   - `:1702-1723` — a second, independent `agent.completed` `json!` literal: the daemon's failure-shaped fallback when a child dies without publishing completion. Slice A single-sourced the fact shape through `Completion::into_fact` (`adapter.rs:199-212`) and pinned it by test — `completion_fact_shape_is_single_sourced` in `tests/codex_adapter_guards.rs` — but that property holds only *within* `crates/rezidnt-run`. This daemon literal agrees key-for-key today and nothing makes it keep agreeing.

**3. The contested item — why Status is PROPOSED.** The codex adapter maps `turn.completed` to `agent.completed{status:"success"}` (`turn_outcome`, `adapter.rs:463`). Two positions, both recorded:
   - *Implementer:* `turn.completed` is the harness positively asserting the turn finished, not an absence being stamped. I6 forbids converting *unknown* into *pass*; the unknown here ("what does a failed turn look like") maps to `None` → no fact. Claude-code likewise derives status from `subtype`/`is_error` with only recorded arms.
   - *Auditor, holding its position, narrowed:* `turn.completed` asserts the turn *terminated*; `agent.completed.status` is the ontology's harness *outcome* — whether it terminated well. Those are two propositions and the codex line carries one. The claude-code comparison cuts the other way: there `type:"result"` carries termination while `subtype`/`is_error` carry outcome separately — exactly why that mapping is a derivation and this one is an inference. Slice A's `turn_outcome()` refactor is byte-identical in behavior; it makes the assumption legible and gives a future arm a home, but it documents the judgment call rather than resolving it.
   - *Evidence, precisely stated:* `spec/fixtures/transcripts/codex_exec_v0.145.0.jsonl` line 3 is an `item.completed` of type `error` (benign skills-context noise) followed by `turn.completed` at line 5. This is **consistent with** the mapping; it does not **establish** it — no recorded transcript shows a turn that ended badly.
   - *The residual, precisely:* the unmapped-failure gap is already covered — the daemon's fallback (`runs.rs:1702`, keyed on `completed_id.is_none()`) turns a codex run that fails *without* emitting `turn.completed` into an honest error on the fabric. The open branch is **mismapped** failure: if codex ever emits `turn.completed` for a turn that ended badly, the adapter publishes `success` and the fallback never fires — a completion was already published.

## Decision

1. **Record, don't fix, the version-gate reachability gap now.** Slice A's own criteria (claude-code green, codex gates correctly *when called*) stand met; wiring the gate into a live daemon call site is a separate, undecided piece of work. *(Alternative — retroactively require slice A to also wire the gate before this record — lost: it would be scope creep past DR-048 §Decision 6's file boundary, decided after the fact, and a rushed call-site fix is exactly the class of remediation-introduces-a-new-defect pattern DR-047 named eleven instances of.)*

2. **Slice B (DR-048's trial matrix primitive) and any daemon wiring it requires MAY NOT close until the three `runs.rs` traps above are fixed at the call site**, added here as entry criteria DR-048 did not name: (a) the `pep` stamp is keyed on `plan.permit_hook_config().is_some()`, not gate-name presence; (b) `AdapterError::ContractViolated` is excluded from the tolerant-warn branch and surfaces as a fact-worthy failure; (c) the second `agent.completed` fallback literal is pinned against `Completion::into_fact`'s shape by a cross-crate test, not left to agree by coincidence. *(Alternative — leave these as generic backlog debt for whoever next touches `runs.rs` — lost: this project has repeatedly shown "someone will notice" is how silent-wrong output ships; DR-047 counted eleven instances of one defect class, three introduced by remediation itself, and two of these three traps are exactly that shape — a fact that looks true and is wired wrong.)*

3. **The codex `turn.completed → status:"success"` mapping is left AS BUILT for now, flagged not settled.** Entry-gated: before any Trials scoring path (DR-048 slice D, the winner-take-all gate) trusts a codex `"success"` verdict, a failing or errored codex transcript must be recorded and mapped, converting the open question into a tested arm — cheap (minutes: `codex exec` with a bogus `--model`, or an interrupted turn). *(Alternative — resolve the mapping now by fiat, either direction — lost: the auditor's narrowing is not dismissed, the recorded evidence is consistent-with, not dispositive, and a wrong call here is invisible under I6's own pass/fail contract — a mismapped failing turn would score as `pass`, the exact miscoercion I6 exists to forbid. The owner should make this call knowingly, the same principle DR-048/049 flagged their own ratifications by.)*

**Acceptance criteria for this record:** owner reviews and either ACCEPTS as PROPOSED-then-ratified or overturns Decision 3's default (leave-as-built). Acceptance criteria for slice B itself are Decision 2's three conditions plus the existing DR-048 §Decision 4 scope, gauntlet green (`/vet` + `/debrief`), nothing else.

> **Decision 3 is CLOSED** — settled by evidence, not by default. The criterion immediately above resolved on the **ACCEPT** branch when the owner accepted [DR-051](DR-051-codex-failure-recording-and-fallback-fidelity.md) (2026-07-25): a real failing codex-cli 0.145.0 transcript was recorded (`spec/fixtures/transcripts/codex_exec_v0.145.0_turn_failed.jsonl`) and terminates in `turn.failed`, never `turn.completed`, so the entry gate Decision 3 imposed is **DISCHARGED** and the `turn.completed → status:"success"` mapping is CONFIRMED — **scoped to `TESTED_CODEX_VERSIONS = &[(0,145)]` only**, never generalized; any other codex version needs its own recording. The auditor's structural dissent survives as accurate description of codex's one-channel outcome signal, not as an open risk for this version window. Decision 3's text above is UNCHANGED — this is its closing annotation.
